Scoring guide

Each attack is scored out of 100: Stars (50) + Destruction (30) + Execution (20). Stars: 3★=50, 2★=35, 1★=15, 0★=0. Destruction: 3 points per complete 10% (maximum 30). Execution is entered by the evaluator: 0, 5, 10, 15 or 20.
